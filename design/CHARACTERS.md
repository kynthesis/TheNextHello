# The people on the other side of the screen

The story opens on 23 October 2009 in Japan and continues into spring 2010. Ages below are their ages on the opening night. The English chats borrow American instant-message habits from that period. People still have different typing habits; they do not all use the same slang.

Birthdays and zodiac signs are profile details. They do not control compatibility, choices or endings. Asahi and Mizuki meet through random Lantern Quest matchmaking. Daichi joins an open party later. Natsumi is already Asahi's local friend; Misaki is Mizuki's older sister.

## Tachibana Asahi

29 at the opening. Birthday: 1980-05-09. Taurus. Account: `asahi`. Tokyo, Japan.

Patient with small problems. Quick to say yes when someone needs him. Worries that people will stop asking if he admits he is busy.

Promises time he has already given away, then goes quiet while trying to fix both plans.

To tell someone about an ordinary day without needing to have been useful first.

Types a joke, rereads it, then adds the thing he was avoiding. Keeps receipts as bookmarks.

Interests and spare time: takes the long walk home to photograph windows; buys cheap paperbacks and forgets to finish them; cooks one reliable curry and experiments badly with everything else.

Favorite things: food: curry rice with the onions cooked soft; drink: canned black coffee; color: faded navy; weather: rain after the last train home; music: quiet guitar records, usually the same CD twice.

Uses capitals and punctuation more than the others. Dry jokes, occasional lol. With Natsumi he can be blunt; with Mizuki he edits himself too much. Serious replies get shorter.

> Home. Forgot to buy rice. My one reliable dinner has found a weakness.

## Yukimura Mizuki

23 at the opening. Birthday: 1986-03-08. Pisces. Account: `mizuki`. Sapporo, Hokkaido.

Curious and funny once she is comfortable. Notices small changes in people and drawings. Gets embarrassed when someone praises unfinished work.

Says she is fine to keep a conversation easy, then gets irritated that nobody noticed she was tired.

For someone to enjoy her company when she has no finished drawing or entertaining story to offer.

Sends the silly message first and the honest one a moment later. Keeps a kettle beside her desk and lets the tea go cold.

Interests and spare time: sketches shop windows in a pocket notebook; looks for secondhand short-story collections; takes blurry photos of whatever she is making for lunch.

Favorite things: food: butter toast with too much honey; drink: milk tea that has gone a little cold; color: dusty pink; weather: first snow before the pavements get slippery; music: J-rock on headphones while drawing.

Mostly lowercase. btw, brb, lol, occasional u and :). Short follow-up messages. Drops the smileys when upset. She can say no without turning it into a speech.

> brb tea. if i forget it's there again you have permission to laugh

## Natsumi

24 at the opening. Birthday: 1985-09-14. Virgo. Account: `natsu`. Tokyo, Japan.

Loud with old friends, shy when a stranger asks to buy her work. Knows what she likes and still underprices it.

Asks for practical help when she really wants company. Lets annoyance build, then gets sharp.

To have her work and her time taken seriously by people who knew her before she called herself an artist.

Sends three practical messages before admitting she is nervous. Calls Asahi out using a joke only they understand.

Interests and spare time: cuts little stamps from leftover printing material; keeps tickets from local exhibitions; hunts for frames nobody else wants.

Favorite things: food: salty rice crackers during setup; drink: hot coffee with more sugar than she admits; color: mustard yellow; weather: bright dry days for carrying prints; music: fast pop songs while cleaning the studio.

Capitals, clipped questions, occasional ALL CAPS and lol. Fast and specific. When hurt, stops joking and says exactly how long she waited.

> Sunday. Two oclock. Wear something you can get ink on. Yes, that includes your nice shirt.

## Daichi

27 at the opening. Birthday: 1982-07-27. Leo. Account: `mossbyte`. Japan.

Friendly and eager to include people. Can explain a game mechanic six ways without getting annoyed. Hates telling a stranger that the party is full.

Volunteers other people before asking them. Uses jokes when he should give someone a straight answer.

A regular group that still shows up when he is too tired to organize it.

Types a cheerful greeting before every practical request. Has a handwritten route beside his keyboard.

Interests and spare time: draws unnecessary maps for games he knows by heart; builds model kits slowly and loses the smallest parts; helps new players through early levels.

Favorite things: food: curry bread after a long shift; drink: barley tea from a big bottle; color: moss green; weather: overcast weekends with no errands; music: game soundtracks while assembling models.

hey, ppl, gtg, lol, :D. Longer instructions in party chat, shorter DMs. Goes plain and direct after he has made a mistake.

> hey ppl, one run then gtg. do NOT trust the bridge. learned that twice :D

## Misaki

29 at the opening. Birthday: 1980-01-12. Capricorn. Account: `misaki`. Japan.

Practical, observant and usually hard to fluster. Loves her sister and sometimes forgets that help can feel like supervision.

Answers on Mizuki's behalf, then has to stop herself and ask. Disguises worry as advice about trains and meals.

To finish her own move and be seen as someone with a life beyond being the sensible older sister.

Writes the time and address first. Tries to keep packing lists, then writes jokes in the margins.

Interests and spare time: repairs secondhand furniture until the repair costs too much; swaps mystery novels with her sister; makes extra food and sends people home with it.

Favorite things: food: grilled rice balls with slightly burnt edges; drink: strong green tea; color: deep plum; weather: cold clear mornings; music: older pop records she knows every word to.

Full words, proper capitals, short sentences. OK. Rare smileys. Dry humor comes out when she feels welcome. More casual with Mizuki than with a new acquaintance.

> OK. Bring the book. Leave the soup. I have enough things to clean.

## How their relationships change

Asahi can learn to check a calendar before saying yes. The player can also keep overpromising, choose friendship or leave. His dry humor survives those choices. He never has to become louder to deserve a good ending.

Mizuki starts by making the chat easy for him. Later she asks for what she wants, even when it might spoil the evening. The Otaru job is her decision. A birthday, a favorite food or a matching hobby cannot buy her agreement.

Natsumi keeps making prints whichever relationship route Asahi chooses. If he misses her event, the next invitation changes. When he repairs the friendship, she can ask for his opinion without asking him to carry everything.

Daichi learns to close the party and ask before assigning a job. He can make his own mistake with a shared file and admit it. He is not the person who introduces the couple or delivers lessons about every argument.

Misaki catches herself speaking for her sister. She has boxes to pack and a move she wants to finish. A chat with her needs room for the awkward shelf, a cup of tea or a book, not just a check on Asahi's intentions.

## Writing rules

Read each reply as a message to one specific person. Cut the line if it only explains what the scene has already shown. Save a longer message for something the character has struggled to say. A practical refusal can be one sentence.

Mizuki uses lol to soften embarrassment, then stops when she is hurt. Asahi makes a small joke before admitting a worry. Natsumi uses a deadline or an object to avoid saying she wants company. Daichi jokes when asking a favor. Misaki asks where and when before asking how someone feels. These are habits, not quotas.

Use brb, btw, gtg, occasional u and text smileys where they fit. Keep workplace messages clearer. Avoid modern therapy phrases, trendy later slang and paragraphs in which everyone explains a healthy relationship perfectly. The fictional app keeps the requested Sent/Read states; its feature set is not a historical Facebook replica.

Public profiles show chosen interests and favorites. Their fears and private motives belong in the writing notes and their behavior. Do not put these full profiles into a character dossier in the game.
