# The phone: essential apps

**Creator direction:** Keep only essential apps and use names whose function is immediately clear. The phone belongs to Asahi, and its purpose is to convey long-distance love. Companion to [the GDD](GDD.md).

## Seven essential apps

| App | Function | Story connection |
|---|---|---|
| **Messages** | Direct/group chats, call transcripts, drafts and explicit replies. | Closeness, boundaries, consent and meaningful decisions. |
| **Games** | Lantern Quest, party conversation and cooperative encounters. | Asahi and Mizuki meet and build shared routines. |
| **Work** | Team messages, handoffs, leave and availability decisions. | Independent responsibilities affect calls and meeting plans. |
| **Photos** | Original photos, screenshots, descriptions and shared memories. | Everyday moments and privacy have lasting context. |
| **Calendar** | Commitments, availability and travel plans. | Agreements and scheduling changes remain visible. |
| **Notes** | Chapters, first-person reminders, next-action links and completed entries. | Guidance follows the current run without exposing unchosen paths. |
| **Settings** | Asahi's account, display, sound, motion and saves. | Essential preferences and recoverable progress. |

All seven apps fit on one Home page. There is no duplicate dock, decorative wallpaper or oversized clock. Keep the interface minimal so the story remains the focus. Labels accompany icons. Lantern Quest is the title shown within Games, rather than the name of an ambiguous launcher app. Work replaces Desk and Photos replaces Gallery throughout navigation, notifications and story links.

## What is removed

Moments, Resonance, Vista, Loop and Bookmark are no longer standalone apps. Their social feeds, music library, video/storyboard library and reading library are removed from the playable phone. Shared interests can still be expressed in authored Messages scenes. A buddy-read or shared song does not require another browsing app. Preserve the three narrative paths and existing consequential choices.

## Personal phone and connected state

Home shows Asahi's local clock and Tokyo location. Notes uses his voice; Settings contains his account. All UI stays inside the phone. Photos and Calendar derive updates from the same committed story state as Messages, Games and Work. Opening an app never advances time, duplicates an event or sends a draft.

Notes only lists this run's completed events and links to the app containing the next action. At the end of the opening it explicitly indicates the playable boundary. It never promises an unimplemented action.

Images keep canonical IDs, captions and privacy context. Essential context remains available through direct links and text descriptions; players never search a collection for an invisible progression flag.

## Saving and compatibility

Existing saves translate Lantern Quest to Games, Desk to Work and Gallery to Photos, including notifications, unread badges and per-app scroll. A save last open in a removed app returns to Home. Story position, drafts, facts and delivered messages are preserved. Older optional-library data remains in the compatible save shape but has no active app or playback control.

## Controls and verification

One standard text size, keyboard focus, visible Home controls, mouse/touch scrolling, notification history, reduced motion remain. Phone proportions stay fixed at 414:896 across portrait and wide windows. For each app, verify navigation, useful content, valid outgoing links and saved state. Check all three story paths, older saves and notification deep links after renaming apps.
